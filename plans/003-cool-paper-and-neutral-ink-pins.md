## Why this matters

The owner is moving the visual direction to a navy ground with gold action. The
semantic roles must NOT change meaning: `paper` stays the background and surface
ramp in both modes, and `ink` stays the foreground and text ramp in both modes.
So the navy has to come from the `paper` ramp itself: its light steps get a cool
navy undertone, and its dark end (950, the dark-mode page background) becomes
deep navy. Ink currently leans warm brown (chroma about 0.03, hue about 69),
which looks muddy on a cool paper, so ink becomes a neutral with only a trace of
cool tint. No role in `roles.css` is edited by this plan; the theme shifts
because the primitives it points at shift.

## Current state

- `scripts/color-ramps.mjs` — generates the primitive ramps into
  `src/styles/palette.css` and `src/styles/palette.generated.json`. Pins are
  exact brand values; steps between and beyond pins are interpolated in OKLCH
  (lightness, chroma and hue all linear between two pins; beyond the last pin,
  lightness runs to `DARKEST` and chroma decays).
- `src/styles/roles.css` — hand-edited semantic roles pointing at ramp steps.
  Read-only for this plan.
- `figma/manifest.json` — generated hand-off for Figma, contains a hash of the
  palette and roles. Regenerated by `pnpm figma-manifest`.

## Maintenance notes

- The owner hand-tunes pins. The mechanism is: edit pins in
  `scripts/color-ramps.mjs`, run `pnpm color-ramps`, `pnpm check:contrast`, then
  `pnpm figma-manifest` and ask for a Figma re-sync.
- `paper-950` (`#00041b`) currently equals `navy-950`. That is deliberate for
  now (the navy concept used navy-950 as the dark background). The `navy` ramp
  remains the saturated blue for the focus ring and future blue marks.
- Interpolation makes paper's chroma rise toward the dark end (about 0.010 at 100
  to 0.056 at 950), so light-mode mid steps (border, line, hover) are a cool
  slate. If that reads too blue, the fix is to add one more pin (for example at
  500), not to change roles.
- Any file that copies ink hex values by hand (favicon and logo SVG) must be
  re-synced after ink changes; plan 008 covers that.
